Privacy

Your call goes straight to the people in it.

Audio and video flow directly between browsers. Our server only helps participants find each other.

  • hub
    Direct WebRTC connections
    Media travels peer-to-peer whenever your network allows it.
  • encrypted
    Encrypted in transit
    Every stream is protected with DTLS-SRTP, built into WebRTC.
  • memory
    Effects stay on your device
    Background blur and replacement are processed locally.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?add
No. Guests just type a name and join. Signing in keeps your display name and avatar with you across devices.
Do participants have to install anything?add
Nothing at all. YukiMeet runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop and mobile.
How do I invite people?add
Every room has its own link (/j/room-name). Tap the link icon inside a call to copy it, or create a scheduled invitation from your profile page.
Is my video sent to a server for background blur?add
No. Blur and background replacement run locally in your browser; only the finished video is sent to the other participants.
What happens on a weak connection?add
If your bandwidth drops, YukiMeet offers to switch your camera off so your voice keeps coming through clearly.
